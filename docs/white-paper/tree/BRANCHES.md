## 0. Why these are branches and not leaves of the GS tree

The 3-year strategy fixes the PhD compendium as GS (the paradigm) + Loom (formal methods / MDE) + Chronicle (information systems / knowledge management), with BioIso as the self-evolution line, and the hygiene rule that the thesis rests on separable artifacts and not on the commercial governance product. Each branch therefore has its own claim, its own evidence and its own venues. They connect to the GS root but do not depend on it.

Independence rules (the dilution guard, from `strategy-3yr.md` section 2, "Regla de dilucion"; the risk is content, not calendar):

1. Evidence flows one way only where it exists. The GS root may cite a branch result as a labelled tier row (for example ALX as a formal-tier demonstration, tier D). A branch paper may cite GS as motivation (the derivability constraint as a design requirement). No branch claim is conditional on a GS result and no GS claim is conditional on a branch result.
2. GS is not reshaped to pass a branch's review, and a branch is not reshaped to carry GS's claims. Each branch is judged on its own field's standards (language design, optimization, information systems).
3. The same "no claim beyond its tier" rule applies. Branch experiments live in the Loom repository, outside the GS logbook; before any is cited they get a logbook-format entry with outcome label and tier (the ALX precedent: backfilled, tier D, "primary record unchecked").
4. Shared namespaces are disambiguated in print. The BIOISO paper already carries a note that its T1 to T5 optimizer tiers are not the GS cascade tiers; keep it.
5. Private material: Invellum holds NDA material according to the personal-repos note; check before any Invellum-based result is public.

## 1. LOOM branch (formal methods / model-driven engineering)

**Branch-root claim (L0), stated at its honest strength.** Loom makes five long-known semantic disciplines (units of measure, information-flow labels, typestate lifecycles, algebraic operation properties, privacy and regulatory labels) first-class constructs of one language that projects a single source to several targets (Rust, TypeScript, WebAssembly, OpenAPI, JSON Schema), designed so a reader with no memory can derive correct output from it. Loom's own state-of-the-project brief says none of the five is new (each is 30 to 50 years old) and that the novelty is the combination, the keyword-level first-class treatment, the multi-target projection and the design for an AI reader. The claim is therefore a language-design claim, not an empirical claim about AI.

## 3. CHRONICLE branch (information systems / knowledge management)

**Candidate branch-root claim, for JC to confirm or change.** A tiered, decaying, trigger-firing persistent memory server (MCP) gives otherwise stateless assistants continuity across sessions. The repository states the design (tiers, relevance ranking, reinforcement and decay, pre-action triggers, session recovery, cloud sync, team extension) and keeps an execution-record directory (`docs\evidence\`: build, lint, mutation, MCP smoke, isolation and cloud-sync checks, non-functional benchmarks on two machines, dated 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-13) governed by the rule that no status may read "passing" without a record.

- **Natural controlled comparison, owed:** continuity and reconstruction. In P3 the cheapest rival to the substrate is git history with change text as the ledger (arm A7); a memory-server arm would be a further comparator, but adding it to P3 changes a preregistered design (structural change).
