Method: two fresh stateless `claude -p` runs (model opus, no tools, no session persistence, no slash commands, no settings sources, run from a neutral directory) that saw only a critic prompt adapted from the fixed prompt of `COPILOT-CRITIC-RUNBOOK.md` (both-direction review of an author-side comparison, the eight examination items, quote-and-fix rules, severity scale), a lens line, the draft file and the simulation results, inline. They did not see the protocol, the sibling designs, `simulate.py`, the formulas, the spec-kit repository or `method-vs-enforcement-2026-10-07.md`, so they could not check consistency with those; they said so in their own "could not assess" lists.

**Independence statement, plainly.** Both critics are the same vendor family (Anthropic) as the author of the draft; they share its training and blind spots, and neither is a person. **Vendor-diverse review is owed**: at least three vendors, two not Anthropic (the Copilot-PC pattern is adequate for critics since they need no token counts), the stop rule of `ROLES.md` has not started to count, and the structural changes below reset it in any case. The assistant that wrote the draft also wrote these dispositions; a BLOCKER is not closed on the author's word alone, so JC should read the BLOCKER rows and the raw files. No finding below is a statement that the design is right.

**What the round found, in one paragraph.** Both critics, from different lenses, converged on the same four structural defects, one of which favoured GS and one of which was a trap for GS. (1) The composite outcomes ("GS loses", "spec-kit wins", "bare wins") fired on non-detection and on cost, so that a pilot with no true quality difference would most often publish "GS loses" or "bare wins" (C1-F02, C2-F01): the opposite of the intended even handedness. (2) The public-statement table gave a GS result with an interval including zero the same affirmative sentence as a resolved result, and gave no sentence to the mirror cases (C1-F03, C2-F02): the single most GS-favouring element of the draft. (3) The registered falsifiers were practically unreachable (C1-F04, C2-F07), and the family-wise false-label rate was never added up (C2-F03, C1-F19). (4) The scripted approval default made spec-kit's model-run checks inert while leaving GS's executed gates intact (C1-F01), and the escaped-defect outcome was exposed to vacuous passes and to conditioning on the arm's own earlier success (C2-F04, C1-F11). All were accepted and applied. The critics also caught that the revision 1 simulation printed infeasible rows (an 85% comparator plus 20 points), which inflated the ceiling claims.
