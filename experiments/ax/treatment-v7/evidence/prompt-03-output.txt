### Key design decisions

- **`GET /api/articles` and `/feed`**: `body` field is stripped at the route layer via `toListItem()` per the 2024-08-16 spec change
- **Slug uniqueness**: 8-char random hex suffix prevents collisions on identical titles
- **Viewer context**: Sentinel `NO_VIEWER = -1` used in all Prisma includes ΓÇö avoids conditional include shapes and TypeScript type gymnastics
- **Authorization order**: `updateArticle`/`deleteArticle` check 404 before 403 (existence before permission)
- **`update` re-fetch**: After update, service re-fetches with viewer userId to populate correct `favorited`/`following` context, since title changes regenerate the slug
